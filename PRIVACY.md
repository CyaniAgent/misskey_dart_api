# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-22  
Controller: SakuraCake (individual)  
Package: misskey_dart_api

## 1. The library

`misskey_dart_api` is a client library. It does not include telemetry,
analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs. It does not send data to the
maintainer's servers.

## 2. Requests to Misskey instances

The library sends requests only to the Misskey instance configured by the app
that uses it. The instance operator is an independent data controller; its
privacy policy applies to account data, posts, messages, and files on that
instance.

## 3. Tokens and local data

- The library accepts an access token and sends it in the Misskey request body.
- The library does not persist tokens or user data. Storage is the
  responsibility of the app that uses the library.
- Apps should store tokens in platform secure storage and keep them out of logs,
  analytics, and crash reports.

## 4. GitHub interactions

Information you provide through GitHub issues, PRs, Security Advisories, or the
CLA process is processed by GitHub under GitHub's privacy policy. Do not post
tokens, private messages, or other sensitive data in public issues.

## 5. Purposes and legal bases

The maintainer processes data only to:

- respond to support, security, or CLA requests;
- maintain the package and its repository;
- comply with legal obligations.

Where the Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China
or other laws apply, the legal basis may be performance of a contract, consent,
compliance with a legal obligation, or necessity for security.

## 6. Sharing and retention

- The maintainer does not sell personal information.
- GitHub issues, PRs, and CLA records are retained under GitHub's mechanisms
  unless you request deletion and the law permits it.
- Security reports are retained as long as necessary after the fix and
  disclosure process.

## 7. Your rights

Where applicable law provides, you may access, correct, or request deletion of
personal information you provided on GitHub, or complain to a supervisory
authority. Contact the maintainer through a GitHub Issue; for sensitive
information, use Security Advisories.

## 8. Children

The package is a developer tool and is not directed to children under 13. If
you are below the consent age in your jurisdiction, use it only with the
involvement of a parent or guardian.

## 9. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced in the repository
and the effective date will be updated.

## 10. Contact

- General privacy questions: GitHub Issue (no sensitive data).
- Sensitive requests: GitHub Security Advisories.
